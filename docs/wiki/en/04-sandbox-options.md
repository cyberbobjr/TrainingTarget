# Sandbox options

Page **Training Target** in the sandbox settings.

| Option | Default | Range | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aiming XP per hit | 2.0 | 0 – 100 | Base Aiming XP for each target hit, before target and zone factors ([Experience gained](03-experience.md)). |
| Melee XP per hit | 1.5 | 0 – 100 | Weapon skill XP for each blow on the dummy. |
| Minimum shooting distance | 3 | 1 – 15 | Shots from closer do not count ("Too close to the target"). |
| Hit chance bonus | 0 | −50 – 50 | Percentage points added to the chance to hit. Negative values make training harder. |
| Target sheet durability | 30 | 1 – 500 | Hits a sheet takes before it must be replaced. |
| Dummy durability | 150 | 1 – 2000 | Blows before the dummy must be repaired, counted in baseball bat blows. |
| Cans on the stand | 6 | 1 – 6 | Maximum number of empty cans on the can stand. |
| Melee training | on | — | The dummy gives weapon skill XP. |
| Vanilla targets | on | — | The military targets found in the world can be used. |
| Session timeout (minutes) | 10 | 0 – 240 | Real minutes without a shot before a session ends. 0: never. |

> Capture à faire : Training Target sandbox page.

On a server, these options are stored under `BatmanTT` in the server's sandbox settings.
