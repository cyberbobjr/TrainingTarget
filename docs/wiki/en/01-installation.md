# Installation

1. Subscribe to **Training Target - Shooting Range & Training Dummy** on the Steam Workshop (item 3810261246).
2. In the game's mod selector, enable **batman_Training Target** (`batman_TrainingTarget`). The mod requires Build 42.21 or later.
3. Optional: enable **batman_Training Target - Viewpoint models** (`batman_TrainingTargetViewpoint`) only if you also use [Project Viewpoint](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528). It adds 3D models of the targets, the dummy wear states, the holes and the cans; without Project Viewpoint it does nothing.
4. Start or load a game. The sandbox page **Training Target** lets you tune the mod (see [Sandbox options](04-sandbox-options.md)).

On a server, add `batman_TrainingTarget` to `Mods=` and `3810261246` to `WorkshopItems=` (see [Multiplayer](05-multiplayer.md)).

> Capture à faire : mod selector with batman_Training Target enabled.

Compatible with [SVRP] ClassicBows: its bows and crossbows count on training targets.
