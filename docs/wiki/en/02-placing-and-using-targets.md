# Placing and using targets

## Crafting

All four recipes are in the **Carpentry** category and can be made on any surface. The hammer, saw and writing tool are kept.

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Make Paper Target | hammer, pen/pencil/marker, 1 plank, 2 nails, 1 paper |
| Make Target Stand | hammer, saw, pen/pencil/marker, 3 planks, 4 nails, 1 paper |
| Make Can Stand | hammer, saw, 3 planks, 4 nails |
| Make Training Dummy | hammer, saw, 4 planks, 6 nails, 1 sheet, 10 hay tufts, grass tufts or rags |

Accepted paper: sheet of paper, graph paper, brochure, flier, paperwork, handwritten letter, doodle, kid's doodle, mail.

## Placing

They are regular movable furniture: place them from the inventory, pick them up and move them like any other furniture. A target keeps its wear and its bullet holes when you pick it up and place it again or rotate it, and the dummy keeps its wear state.

- **Paper Target**: on a north or west wall. You must shoot from the side it faces.
- **Target Stand**, **Can Stand**, **Training Dummy**: on the floor, four facings.

> Capture à faire : paper target on a wall, seen from the shooting side.

## Shooting

1. Hold an aimed firearm (or a ClassicBows bow).
2. Stand at least 3 tiles away (sandbox option), on the same floor, within the weapon's range.
3. Aim at the target. With the mouse, the valid-target reticle appears in your target colour and the aim circle goes from red to green with your real chance to hit. With a gamepad, the target is the first one in the direction you face.
4. Shoot. You see **Bullseye!**, **Inner ring**, **Outer ring** or **Missed**, plus a statistics line, and hear the impact.

Your chance to hit follows the game's rules for a zombie: aiming time, sight range, movement, Marksman, arm pain, wind, rain, fog, darkness, moodles, headgear and point blank. The target stand is slightly harder (−5 %), the can stand harder (−15 %).

> Capture à faire : green aim circle over the target stand, with the valid-target reticle.

A sheet wears out after 30 hits by default: "The target sheet is shot to pieces, I should replace it".

### Can stand

Put up to 6 empty cans on the stand (only empty cans are accepted). Each can you hit falls off and is lost. When the stand is empty: "No more cans on the stand".

## Melee training

Hit the training dummy with a melee weapon (axe, long blade, spear, short blade, short blunt or long blunt). Each blow flashes the dummy red and shows the skill, the XP and the dummy's condition, for example "Axe +1.5 XP - dummy 88%". Bare hands and weapons without a category give nothing.

![Melee training on the dummy](../../images/screenshots/dummy-melee-training.png)

The dummy wears according to the weapon: a baseball bat counts as one blow, a knife 0.2, an axe or a sledgehammer 3. Its look changes as it wears; once in tatters it gives no more experience until repaired. Player blows never break training furniture.

## Maintenance and statistics

Right-click the target (or another object on its tile) and open **Training target**:

| Option | Needs |
|---|---|
| State line | Sheet hits, cans on the stand or dummy condition. |
| Replace the sheet | 1 sheet of paper (consumed) and a pen, pencil or marker. Clears wear and holes. Works on vanilla targets too. |
| Repair the dummy | Hammer in hand, 1 plank and 2 nails (consumed). Back to 100 %. |
| Put empty cans on the stand | Empty cans in your inventory. |
| Shooting session statistics | Tooltip: shots, hits, accuracy, bullseyes, best streak. |
| Start a new session | Resets your statistics. |

An option you cannot use stays visible and greyed out, with a tooltip listing what is missing.

> Capture à faire : "Training target" context menu with the statistics tooltip.

During a session your character may say a line: after 3 bullseyes in a row, when beating a best streak of at least 3, or when a miss breaks a streak of 5 or more.

## Vanilla targets

The military shooting targets found in the world (silhouette and circular targets on walls) also count, without drawn holes. An admin can turn this off (see [Sandbox options](04-sandbox-options.md)).
